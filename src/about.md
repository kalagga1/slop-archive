# About the archive

The Slop Archive is a museum for the strange, funny, and uncanny slop that AI systems have released into the world, in every medium. The east wing holds the writing: the LinkedIn post that *delves*, the product listing that loops forever, the poem that runs out of rhymes halfway through. The west wing hangs the pictures: seven-fingered hands, shrimp-based religious art, signs written in a language that almost exists. The screening room plays the videos: AI ads, narrated "facts" channels, and footage that melts if you look at it too long.

The goal is to keep a lighthearted historical record of this moment. Every exhibit says where it was spotted and when, and image and video exhibits come with a written description so the whole collection stays searchable and readable for everyone.

**House rules:** no private people, no punching down, and credit the source when you can. We laugh at the slop, not at the people who got stuck looking at it.

The site is static and free to host. Entries are plain Markdown files in a public repository. Images are kept small, and videos are embedded from YouTube or Vimeo rather than hosted here, which is how admission stays free.

Want a souvenir? Visit the <a href="/shop/">gift shop</a>, or get one fresh specimen a week from <a href="/newsletter/">Slop of the Week</a>.
