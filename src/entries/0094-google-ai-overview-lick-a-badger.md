**Summary:** In April 2025, users found that Google's AI Overviews would explain the "meaning" of made-up sayings. The best-known: "You can't lick a badger twice," which the AI Overview explained as meaning you can't trick or deceive someone a second time after they've already been fooled once. The phrase was invented by the person searching. Google said its systems try to find relevant results even for "nonsensical or false premise" searches.
