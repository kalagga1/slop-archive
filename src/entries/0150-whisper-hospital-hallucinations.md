**Summary:** Researchers and engineers told the AP they had found hallucinations in many Whisper transcripts. In a study of thousands of audio samples, about 1% contained entire hallucinated phrases or sentences, and many of those were harmful. In one transcript cited by AP, Whisper invented a nonexistent medication, "hyperactivated antibiotics." OpenAI had warned against using Whisper in high-risk settings, yet a Whisper-based tool from Nabla was being used by tens of thousands of clinicians, and it deleted the original audio, so errors couldn't be checked.
