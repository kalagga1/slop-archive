**Summary:** Die Aktuelle presented the article as an exclusive interview, with only a small note inside that the quotes were AI-generated. Schumacher's family said they would take legal action. Publisher Funke Mediengruppe apologized, called the article tasteless and misleading, and dismissed editor-in-chief Anne Hoffmann.
