From a CNET explainer on compound interest:

> "if you deposit $10,000 into a savings account that earns 3% interest compounding annually, you'll earn $10,300 at the end of the first year."

**Summary:** You'd earn $300, not $10,300. CNET reviewed all 77 AI-assisted stories and issued corrections on 41 of them. Futurism also found passages that closely resembled earlier work from other outlets.
