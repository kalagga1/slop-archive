**Summary:** Under a column about Anaheim's history with the KKK, the AI-generated "Insights" panel included a point suggesting that some local historical accounts portrayed the 1920s Klan as a product of "white Protestant culture" responding to societal changes rather than an explicitly hate-driven movement. The column was by Gustavo Arellano. Owner Patrick Soon-Shiong had launched the feature to add "different views" to opinion content. The insight was taken down.
