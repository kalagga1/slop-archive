Examples of tortured phrases documented by the researchers:

> "counterfeit consciousness" (artificial intelligence)

> "bosom peril" (breast cancer)

**Summary:** Nature reported that the computer scientists couldn't understand why papers would say "counterfeit consciousness," "profound neural organization" and "colossal information" instead of artificial intelligence, deep neural network and big data. They concluded the text had been run through automatic paraphrasing software, often to disguise plagiarism, and found many such papers in the journal Microprocessors and Microsystems. "Tortured phrases" became a standard red flag for fabricated and paper-mill research.
