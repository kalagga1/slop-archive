**Summary (video described in text):** The clip shows several rabbits taking turns jumping on a trampoline at night, styled like low-resolution home security footage. On close inspection, one rabbit vanishes mid-jump and another changes shape. Snopes reported it passed 230 million views. People shared it as a sign that cheap-looking AI video was now nearly impossible to spot.
