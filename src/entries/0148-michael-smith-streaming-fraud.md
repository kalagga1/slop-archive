**Summary:** Prosecutors said Michael Smith used AI to create hundreds of thousands of songs and used thousands of bot accounts to stream them billions of times, spreading the plays across many tracks so no single song looked suspicious. The case was described as the first of its kind. In March 2026 Smith pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and agreed to forfeit about $8.09 million. In October 2026 he was sentenced to 18 months in prison, according to Rolling Stone.
