**Summary:** The game's producer had mentioned the Midjourney art in an interview, and Square Enix confirmed it. The AI-made images were used as abstract covers for the soundtrack songs players unlock, and the company said everything else was made by its developers. It came as Square Enix's president said the company would be "aggressive" about using AI, which worried artists already angry about AI in games.
