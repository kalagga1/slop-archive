**Summary:** 404 Media and the New York Mycological Society identified several foraging books on Amazon, aimed at beginners, that showed signs of AI generation: authors with no online presence, generic text, and detection-tool scores. The society warned that bad advice could lead someone to eat a deadly mushroom. Amazon removed some titles.
