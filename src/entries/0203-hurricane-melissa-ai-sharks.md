**Summary (video described in text):** Fact-checkers traced a viral clip of a shark in Jamaican floodwater to a TikTok account that labels its work as AI. Other fakes showed news anchors, people partying in the storm and destroyed landmarks. AP reported that many were made with OpenAI's new Sora video app, and experts warned the fakes could pull attention away from real emergency information.
