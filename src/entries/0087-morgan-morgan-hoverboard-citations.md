**Summary:** In a product-liability suit against Walmart and Jetson Electric Bikes, lawyers from Morgan & Morgan submitted motions in limine citing nine cases, eight of which could not be found anywhere. The judge ordered them to explain themselves. The lawyers withdrew the motions and admitted the cases were "hallucinated" by the firm's in-house AI platform. Judge Kelly Rankin fined the drafting attorney $3,000 and removed him from the case, and fined two other lawyers who signed the filing $1,000 each.
