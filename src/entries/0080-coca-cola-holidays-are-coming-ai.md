**Summary (video described in text):** The ads, made with AI studios including Secret Level, Silverside AI and Wild Card, show glowing red Coca-Cola trucks driving through snowy towns, with smiling people and animals that viewers said looked uncanny and plasticky. Critics noted inconsistent truck wheels and faces. Gravity Falls creator Alex Hirsch posted that Coke's red was "made from the blood of out-of-work artists." Coca-Cola released another AI holiday ad in November 2025, which was again criticized.
