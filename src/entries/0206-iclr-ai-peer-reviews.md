**Summary:** Authors had reported reviews that were long and vague, hallucinated citations, or flagged problems that weren't in their papers. Pangram Labs ran its detector over every ICLR 2026 review and estimated 15,899 were fully AI-written, with more than half showing some AI use. Nature reported the findings. ICLR later said it used two detectors and asked area chairs to consider flagged reviews when judging review quality. Some researchers argued the 21% figure may count mixed human and AI text as fully AI-written. This museum's Exhibit 57 covers an earlier study of AI-written peer reviews.
