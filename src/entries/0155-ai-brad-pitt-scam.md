**Summary (image slop described in text):** The woman, identified as Anne, told French broadcaster TF1 that the fake Pitt said his money was tied up in his divorce from Angelina Jolie and that he needed cash for kidney cancer treatment. The edited images of a sickly "Pitt" in hospital were widely mocked online once the story aired. The mockery got so bad that TF1 pulled the segment, and Pitt's representatives warned fans not to respond to online contact from people claiming to be him.
