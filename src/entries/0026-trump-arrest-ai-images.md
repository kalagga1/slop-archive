**Summary (images described in text):** The series showed Trump being chased and wrestled to the ground by officers, then in a prison jumpsuit. Some had telltale AI errors such as distorted hands and garbled badges. Higgins labeled them as fake and said he made them as an experiment while waiting for the arrest. He was later banned from Midjourney.
