**Summary:** Music publishers suing Anthropic flagged a citation in a declaration by data scientist Olivia Chen. Anthropic's law firm, Latham & Watkins, explained that an attorney had used Claude to format the citation. The link pointed to a real article, but Claude supplied an inaccurate title and wrong authors, and a manual check missed it. The lawyers called it "an honest citation mistake and not a fabrication of authority." The judge struck the affected paragraph of the declaration.
