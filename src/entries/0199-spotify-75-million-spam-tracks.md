**Summary:** Spotify said spam included mass uploads, duplicates, keyword-stuffed titles and tracks just over 30 seconds long, the minimum to earn royalties. It announced a spam filter that would stop recommending offending uploads, stricter rules against AI impersonation of real artists, and support for AI-use credits in song metadata. The announcement came months after the AI band in Exhibit 100 and the fake posthumous song in Exhibit 101.
