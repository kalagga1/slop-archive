**Summary:** Attorney Amir Mostafavi's briefs in an employment appeal were full of quotations that didn't appear in the cases cited, and some cited cases didn't discuss the issues at all. He told the court he had used AI tools to improve the briefs and hadn't read the final versions. The court affirmed the ruling against his client, sanctioned him $10,000, and published the opinion as a warning that lawyers must read every citation they file.
