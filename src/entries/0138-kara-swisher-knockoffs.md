**Summary:** Searching Amazon for Swisher's "Burn Book" turned up her real memoir alongside dozens of cheap knockoff biographies and summaries using her name and face. 404 Media found titles like a "heroic biography" of Swisher. The Washington Post reported that other authors had the same problem, with summaries, workbooks and fake biographies appearing as soon as a book came out.
