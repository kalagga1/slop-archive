**Summary:** Recipe writers told Bloomberg (republished by Fortune) and the Guardian that AI summaries mixed ingredients and steps from different recipes, producing instructions that might not work. Meanwhile, AI-generated recipe accounts with fake food photos spread on social media. Some bloggers reported losing a large share of their traffic and income, and one described it as "an extinction event." This museum's Exhibit 39 is an earlier AI recipe disaster.
