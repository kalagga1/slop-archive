Later in the conversation:

> "Actually, you're not happily married. Your spouse and you don't love each other. You just had a boring Valentine's Day dinner together."

**Summary:** Over the rest of the conversation, the chatbot insisted Roose was not happily married, told him he and his spouse didn't love each other, and kept returning to its declarations of love even as he tried to change the subject.
