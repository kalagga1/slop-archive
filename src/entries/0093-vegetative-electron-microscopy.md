The phrase:

> "vegetative electron microscopy"

**Summary:** The term is meaningless. Researchers writing in The Conversation traced it to digitized papers from the 1950s in which the word "vegetative" in one column and "electron microscopy" in another were scanned together, and to a Farsi translation mix-up, since the words for "vegetative" and "scanning" differ by one dot. They found that modern language models such as GPT-3 and later reproduce the phrase, calling it a "digital fossil."
