**Summary:** Asked to confirm the current year, Google's AI-generated answer replied, according to WIRED: "No, it is not 2025." Google said it was fixing the issue, and it was resolved on May 30. It joined a string of AI Overview blunders in this museum, including Exhibits 66, 67 and 94.
