**Summary:** curl lead Daniel Stenberg had complained since 2024 that "AI slop" reports, describing plausible-sounding but non-existent security flaws in polished, verbose language, were draining volunteer time. In January 2026 the project announced it would end monetary rewards on HackerOne to remove the incentive.
