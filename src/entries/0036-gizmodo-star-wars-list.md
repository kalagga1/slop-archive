**Summary:** The article, "A Chronological List of Star Wars Movies & TV Shows," bylined "Gizmodo Bot," listed entries out of order and omitted some series. io9's deputy editor James Whitbrook said he hadn't asked for the article or seen it before publication, and told the Washington Post he had never dealt with "this basic level of incompetence" from colleagues.
