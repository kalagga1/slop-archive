**Summary:** The paper, published in *Physica Scripta* in August 2023, did not disclose any use of ChatGPT. After Cabanac flagged it on PubPeer, the authors confirmed they had used ChatGPT to help draft the manuscript and the publisher retracted it. Nature reported Cabanac had found more than a dozen similar papers.
