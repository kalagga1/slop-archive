**Summary (video described in text):** Ahead of Season 2, the AI recap described the show's flashbacks as taking place in "1950s America." They are actually set in a retro-styled 2077. It also misdescribed a key scene between the main characters. Amazon removed the recaps from Fallout and the other shows in its test.
