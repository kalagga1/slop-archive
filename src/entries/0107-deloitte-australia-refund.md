**Summary:** University of Sydney researcher Chris Rudge found the report for Australia's Department of Employment and Workplace Relations cited non-existent academic papers and attributed a made-up quote to a federal court judgment. Deloitte issued a revised version disclosing that it used a generative AI tool chain based on Azure OpenAI GPT-4o and agreed to repay the final installment of its contract. A Labor senator accused Deloitte of having "a human intelligence problem," according to the Australian Financial Review.
