**Summary:** Judge Julien Xavier Neals withdrew an opinion denying a motion to dismiss after defense counsel identified fabricated quotes attributed to defendants and earlier rulings, and misstated case outcomes. In a later letter to Senator Chuck Grassley, Neals said a law school intern had used ChatGPT without authorization and the draft was posted before normal review. Reuters reported that a Mississippi federal judge also withdrew a ruling that week after attorneys questioned its accuracy.
