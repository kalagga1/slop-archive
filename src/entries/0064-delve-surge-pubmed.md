**Summary:** In 2024, observers noticed papers on PubMed using "delve" far more often than before ChatGPT's release. A study by Dmitry Kobak and colleagues analyzed 14 million PubMed abstracts and found "an abrupt increase in the frequency of certain style words" after LLMs appeared, and estimated that at least 13.5% of 2024 biomedical abstracts were processed with LLMs, reaching 40% for some subsets. The paper's own title, "Delving into LLM-assisted writing," is a joke about the phenomenon.
