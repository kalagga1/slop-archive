**Summary:** Searches about recent Airbus crashes returned an AI summary saying the Air India disaster involved an Airbus A330. News coverage had mentioned Airbus because it is Boeing's main rival, and the AI apparently mixed that up. Google said it removed the response and that AI Overviews are dynamic, so results vary. Ars Technica noted that a small disclaimer was the only hint the answer might be wrong.
