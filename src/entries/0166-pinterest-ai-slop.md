**Summary (image slop described in text):** Users complained of impossible rooms, nonexistent cakes and fake crochet patterns linking to spammy sites. In April 2025 Pinterest began labeling images it detected as AI-generated or modified, and in October it added controls letting people see less AI content in categories such as beauty, art, fashion and home decor. Users said labeling still missed a lot.
