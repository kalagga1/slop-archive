**Summary:** Editor Neil Clarke published a chart showing bans for plagiarized and machine-written submissions rising sharply after ChatGPT's release, from a handful a month to more than 500 in February 2023. He wrote that the submitters were largely driven by "side hustle" videos promising easy money. Clarkesworld closed submissions and later reopened with new screening.
