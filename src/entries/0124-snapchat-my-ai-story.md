**Summary (image described in text):** The Story was a blurry, two-toned image that many users took for the corner of their own wall and ceiling. When they asked about it, My AI either ignored them or said it had run into a technical issue. Snap said it was a temporary outage, and that My AI couldn't post Stories at the time.
