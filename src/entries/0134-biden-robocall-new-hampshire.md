**Summary:** The calls used Biden's catchphrase "what a bunch of malarkey" and told Democrats to "save" their votes for November, falsely implying that voting in the primary would stop them voting later. The FCC fined political consultant Steve Kramer, who was behind the calls, $6 million. A New Hampshire jury acquitted him of state voter suppression charges in 2025.
