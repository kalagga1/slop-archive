**Summary (image described in text):** Jason Allen's entry shows robed figures in an ornate, sunlit, theater-like space looking out through a vast circular window. He generated it with Midjourney, then edited and upscaled it, and submitted it under the name "Jason M. Allen via Midjourney." It won $300 in the emerging digital artists category. Allen told the New York Times, "Art is dead, dude. It's over. A.I. won. Humans lost."
