**Summary (offensive content described, not reproduced):** FN Meka, a CGI rapper with millions of TikTok followers, was promoted as partly AI-generated. Activist group Industry Blackout called the character a "direct insult" and an amalgamation of stereotypes. Posts showed it using a racial slur and depicted it being beaten by police in jail. Capitol Music Group cut ties, apologized to the Black community, and pulled the single "Florida Water."
