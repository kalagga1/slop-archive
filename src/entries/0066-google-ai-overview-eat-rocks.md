**Summary:** Asked how many rocks a person should eat, Google's AI Overview said geologists recommend eating one small rock per day, as reported by the BBC. The source was a 2021 satirical article in *The Onion*, "Geologists Recommend Eating At Least One Small Rock Per Day," which had been republished on a geoscience company's website. Google later said it had restricted satire and humor content in AI Overviews.
