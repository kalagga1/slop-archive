**Summary (video described in text):** Catholic outlet Aleteia reported that the channel "Pope Leo XIV's Sermons" posted 26 videos of AI-voiced homilies over still images of the pope, and had nearly 18,000 subscribers. YouTube removed it for violating its spam and deceptive practices policies. AFP later found more channels posting AI pope content, and YouTube terminated many of them.
