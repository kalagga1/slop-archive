**Summary:** A journalist covering a lawsuit by the Second Amendment Foundation asked ChatGPT to summarize the complaint. ChatGPT produced a summary claiming the complaint accused Mark Walters, described as the foundation's treasurer and chief financial officer, of defrauding and embezzling funds. Walters was not a party to the case, had never held those roles, and no such accusation existed. A Georgia judge granted OpenAI summary judgment in May 2025.
