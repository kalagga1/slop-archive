**Summary:** In a motion asking a judge to end Cohen's court supervision early, his lawyer cited three court decisions that did not exist. Cohen later explained in a filing that he had found them with Google Bard, which he mistook for a kind of search engine, and did not realize the citations were fake. Judge Jesse Furman had flagged that the three cited decisions did not exist.
