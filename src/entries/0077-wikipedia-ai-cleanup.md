**Summary:** Volunteers found articles and edits containing phrases like "as of my last knowledge update," fake references, and occasionally hoax articles about topics that do not exist. Their "Signs of AI writing" guide lists patterns such as promotional puffery, "is a testament to," "pivotal role," and overuse of words like "delve" and "tapestry."
