**Summary (main source is in Dutch):** VRT NWS reported that the fictional "Sophie Vermeulen" was credited with 403 articles, and that other fake writers appeared on Elle Belgium's site as well. Publisher Ventures Media said it had been testing AI-written content, then added AI disclaimers and removed the fake profiles. The Times reported that the same publisher's other magazine titles had used similar AI personas.
