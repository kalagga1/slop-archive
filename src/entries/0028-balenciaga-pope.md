**Summary (image described in text):** The image showed Pope Francis walking outside in a large, white, quilted puffer coat with a jeweled crucifix. It was created by a 31-year-old construction worker from Chicago using Midjourney,. It spread rapidly after being posted on the r/midjourney subreddit.
