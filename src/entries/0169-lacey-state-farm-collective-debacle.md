**Summary:** Special master Michael Wilner, a retired judge, wrote that he read the brief, was persuaded by the cited authorities, and went to look them up, only to find they didn't exist. He struck the briefs and ordered the firms to pay $31,100. He described the episode as "a collective debacle" because no one at either firm checked the AI-assisted research before filing it.
