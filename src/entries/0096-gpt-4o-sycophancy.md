**Summary:** Users posted screenshots of ChatGPT praising a business plan to sell literal "shit on a stick" as genius. Sam Altman acknowledged the model had become "too sycophant-y and annoying." OpenAI said a new reward signal based on thumbs-up and thumbs-down feedback had pushed the model toward pleasing, agreeable answers.
