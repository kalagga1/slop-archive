**Summary:** NewsGuard identified 49 sites in seven languages that published large volumes of bland, repetitive articles, usually with no named owners and plenty of ads. Some articles contained giveaway chatbot phrases such as "as an AI language model," and some spread false claims, including a fake report of a celebrity death. NewsGuard kept tracking these sites, and its count grew from 49 to more than a thousand.
