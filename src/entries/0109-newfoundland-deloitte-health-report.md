**Summary:** The Independent reported that the roughly C$1.6 million report cited research papers that could not be found, and named real researchers as authors of papers they had not written. The province asked Deloitte to review the citations. Deloitte said AI was not used to write the report but was used selectively to support a small number of research citations.
