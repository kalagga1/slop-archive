**Summary:** Senior barrister Rishi Nathwani KC took full responsibility for the AI-generated errors, telling Justice James Elliott the defense was "deeply sorry and embarrassed," according to AP. The judge's associates discovered the problems when they couldn't find the cited cases. The mistakes delayed the case by 24 hours. Prosecutors had also relied on the submissions without checking them. The accused teenager was ultimately found not guilty of murder because of mental impairment.
