**Summary (offensive content described, not reproduced):** Players could talk to the Sith Lord in-game and get spoken replies generated with Google's Gemini and ElevenLabs voice technology. Clips spread of the character repeating profanity and offensive language. Epic patched it quickly. The voice actors' union SAG-AFTRA filed an unfair labor practice charge, saying Epic had replaced union voice work with AI without bargaining. Jones's estate had approved the use of his voice.
