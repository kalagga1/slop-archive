The line found in chapter 3 of the published book:

> "I've rewritten the passage to align more with J. Bree's style, which features more tension, gritty undertones, and raw emotional subtext beneath the supernatural elements:"

From author Lena McDonald's apology, as reproduced by Screen Rant:

> "The truth is, I used AI to help edit and shape parts of the book."

**Summary:** The passage was quietly removed from the Amazon edition, but screenshots spread on Reddit and Goodreads. 404 Media noted it was not the first such case that year. Another romance author, K.C. Crowne, had also been caught with an AI prompt in a book.
