A string found in several of the report's reference URLs:

> "oaicite"

**Summary:** NOTUS first reported that at least seven cited studies did not exist. The Washington Post and others then found that some references included "oaicite," a tag associated with OpenAI's tools, suggesting AI was used to generate citations. The White House called the problems "formatting issues" and updated the report.
