**Summary (video described in text):** A Deadline investigation found that Screen Culture had made 23 versions of a trailer for The Fantastic Four: First Steps, some of which outranked the official trailer in YouTube search. It also reported that studios including Warner Bros. Discovery and Sony had asked YouTube to direct the ad revenue from such videos to them. YouTube cut off their ad revenue in March 2025. After they were allowed back into the monetization program and kept using misleading titles, YouTube terminated Screen Culture and KH Studio in December 2025.
