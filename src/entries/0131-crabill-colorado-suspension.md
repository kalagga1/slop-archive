**Summary:** Zachariah Crabill filed a motion in a civil case citing cases ChatGPT had produced, without checking them. According to the disciplinary stipulation, he discovered before the hearing that the cases were wrong or fictitious but didn't alert the court or withdraw the motion, and when the judge asked, he blamed an intern. Colorado's presiding disciplinary judge approved a suspension of a year and a day, with 90 days served and the rest stayed during two years of probation.
