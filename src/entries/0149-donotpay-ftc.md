**Summary:** According to The Verge, DoNotPay had said it would "replace the $200-billion-dollar legal industry with artificial intelligence." The FTC said the company hadn't tested the quality and accuracy of most of its legal documents and advice, and hadn't employed attorneys to check them. Under the final order, announced in February 2025, the company paid $193,000, had to notify 2021 to 2023 subscribers, and was barred from claiming its product works like a real lawyer without evidence. The case was part of the FTC's "Operation AI Comply."
