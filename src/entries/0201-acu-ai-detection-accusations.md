**Summary:** The ABC spoke to students, including nursing students, who had results withheld for months while they tried to prove they wrote their own work, sometimes affecting graduate job offers. ACU said the detector was never the only evidence, but students told the ABC otherwise. The university stopped using the AI detection tool in March 2025.
