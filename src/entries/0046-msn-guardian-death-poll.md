**Summary:** Beside the Guardian's report on the death of Lilie James, MSN's automated "Insights" feature asked readers: "What do you think is the reason behind the woman's death?" with options of murder, accident or suicide. Readers assumed the Guardian had written it. Guardian Media Group's chief executive wrote to Microsoft president Brad Smith saying it caused significant reputational damage.
