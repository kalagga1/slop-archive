**Summary:** The Markup tested the Microsoft-powered MyCity chatbot and found it gave wrong and illegal advice: it said bosses could take a cut of workers' tips, that landlords did not have to accept Section 8 housing vouchers, and that businesses could go cashless, all contrary to New York law. Mayor Eric Adams defended keeping it online.
