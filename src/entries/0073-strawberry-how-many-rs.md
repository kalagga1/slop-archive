**Summary:** Models including GPT-4o and Claude would answer that "strawberry" has two R's, and some argued with users who corrected them. The cause is tokenization: models see chunks of words rather than individual letters. OpenAI's reasoning model, released in September 2024, was codenamed Strawberry, which many took as a nod to the meme.
