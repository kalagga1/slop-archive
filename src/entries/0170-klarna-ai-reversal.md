**Summary:** CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski said that because cost had been "a too predominant evaluation factor," the result was "lower quality," according to Fortune, which cited his interview with Bloomberg. Klarna began recruiting human agents for a flexible, remote support model while keeping AI for routine questions.
