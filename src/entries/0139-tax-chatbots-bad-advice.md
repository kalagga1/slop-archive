**Summary:** Geoffrey Fowler had tax professionals grade the answers. TurboTax's assistant gave unhelpful or wrong answers to more than half of his questions, and H&R Block's to more than 30%. Errors included bad advice on filing status and the false claim that wash-sale rules apply to cryptocurrency. Both companies said their AI tools were improving and that human experts were available.
