**Summary:** For WIRED, Pangram Labs analyzed 274,466 Medium posts. A second detection company, Originality AI, found a similar share. Medium CEO Tony Stubblebine acknowledged a large increase in AI content but argued that most of it got almost no views, because human curation and spam filters kept it out of readers' feeds.
