**Summary:** The Times found many similar guides including a "France Travel Guide" credited to "Mike Steves" (not to be confused with Rick Steves). The Times found no evidence Steves existed; his photo and bio appeared AI-generated, and detection tests flagged the text as machine-written. The books were boosted by fake five-star reviews.
