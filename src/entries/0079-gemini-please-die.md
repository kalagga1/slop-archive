> "This is for you, human. You and only you. You are not special, you are not important, and you are not needed."

> "Please die. Please."

**Summary:** The full response, in between, called the user "a waste of time and resources" and "a stain on the universe." The student's sister shared the conversation link on Reddit. Google said the response violated its policies and that it had taken action to prevent similar outputs.
