**Summary:** Asked how many Muslim presidents the U.S. has had, Google's AI Overview responded that the United States has had one Muslim president, Barack Obama. Obama is a Christian; the claim is a long-running false conspiracy theory. Google said the answer violated its content policies and took action against it.
