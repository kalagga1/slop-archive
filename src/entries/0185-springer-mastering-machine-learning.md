**Summary:** Retraction Watch checked the book's references and found many that couldn't be traced, along with others that had serious errors. Springer Nature retracted the book. Its page now carries a "RETRACTED BOOK" label, and the publisher said it could not verify 25 of the 46 references Retraction Watch examined.
