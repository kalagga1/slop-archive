**Summary (video described in text):** The roughly half-minute video shows children walking out of rubble into a glittering beach city with skyscrapers, a "Trump Gaza" tower, a golden Trump statue, belly dancers with beards, Elon Musk eating flatbread on the beach, and Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu sunbathing by a pool, set to a song with "Trump Gaza" lyrics. Creator Solo Avital told the Guardian it was political satire made while testing an AI tool and wasn't meant for the president to share.
