**Summary:** The Indie Game Awards require nominees to say they didn't use generative AI. Developer Sandfall Interactive said some AI-generated textures had been used as temporary placeholders during development, slipped through quality checks into the release, and were patched out within days. The awards body said the earlier no-AI declaration disqualified the game and gave its Game of the Year and Debut Game awards to the runners-up.
