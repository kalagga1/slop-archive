**Summary (video described in text):** Viewers pointed to distorted hands and faces, creatures that morphed between frames, and a flat AI voiceover. Snail Games said its marketing team made the trailer from game assets with AI animation tools, and that the development team wasn't involved and hadn't used AI to build the expansion.
