**Summary:** In a widely shared TikTok, two customers begged the drive-thru AI to stop as it kept adding Chicken McNuggets to their order, eventually reaching 260. Other videos showed it adding bacon to ice cream and unrequested butter packets. McDonald's announced in June 2024 that it would end the IBM partnership test, while saying voice ordering would remain part of its future.
