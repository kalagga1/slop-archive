**Summary:** In April 2025 Deezer said about 18% of the tracks uploaded each day, more than 20,000, were fully AI-generated. By September the share was 28%, and by November about 50,000 tracks a day, roughly a third of uploads. Deezer tagged AI albums, kept them out of its editorial playlists, and said many fully AI tracks were uploaded to farm royalties with fake streams. It also published a survey in which most listeners couldn't tell AI music from human-made music.
