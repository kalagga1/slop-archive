**Summary:** CBC found that the Education Accord NL report cited non-existent sources, including a 2008 National Film Board movie that does not exist. One of the citations appeared to be copied from a style guide's example of a fake reference. The co-chairs said errors would be fixed. University faculty said the fabrications were consistent with AI-generated citations.
