**Summary:** Instructor Jared Mumm gave students "X" (incomplete) grades after ChatGPT claimed authorship of their papers, briefly putting seniors' diplomas in doubt. Students showed timestamped Google Docs to prove they wrote their work. The university said no one ultimately failed or was barred from graduating, and that it was investigating and writing AI policies. Reporters showed that ChatGPT will also "admit" to writing text it never wrote.
