**Summary:** Hoodline, a once-respected San Francisco local news brand, was relaunched by new owner Impress3 with AI-generated articles credited to "personas." A small "AI" badge sat next to the bylines. Nieman Lab found that the fake names often matched the ethnic makeup of the city they covered, and some articles contained errors and apparent hallucinations.
