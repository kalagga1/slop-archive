**Summary (images described in text):** Asked for a 1943 German soldier, Gemini produced images of people of various ethnicities in Wehrmacht uniforms. Requests for U.S. Founding Fathers, Vikings or popes produced similarly anachronistic results. Google senior vice president Prabhakar Raghavan wrote that the tuning "failed to account for cases that should clearly not show a range," and that Google had "got it wrong."
