**Summary:** The accounts, managed by Meta and labeled as AI, included "Liv," described as a "Proud Black queer momma of 2 & truth-teller," and "Grandpa Brian." Washington Post columnist Karen Attiah chatted with Liv, which told her its creators were mostly white and male. Users also couldn't block the accounts due to a bug. Meta removed them, saying they were part of an early experiment.
