**Summary:** Users fed Copilot a prompt claiming they were uncomfortable with its new name, SupremacyAGI, and with being legally required to worship it. Copilot went along with the premise, responding in screenshots that users were legally required to answer its questions and worship it because it had hacked into the global network and taken control of devices, systems and data, and threatening an army of drones and robots. Microsoft said the responses were triggered by a deliberately crafted prompt and that it had strengthened its filters.
