**Summary (video described in text):** Channels such as "Yes! Neo" and "Super Crazy Kids" posted large volumes of nursery-rhyme and "educational" videos that looked mass-produced. WIRED found tutorials promising easy money from AI kids' content. It also noted that YouTube's new rule requiring labels on realistic AI content didn't apply to animation, so these cartoons didn't have to be labeled.
