**Summary (video described in text):** The clip shows a woman arguing with a gate agent while a kangaroo in a harness holds a ticket in its paws. Fact-checkers pointed to telltale signs: gibberish dialogue, unreadable text on the boarding pass, and a ring that appears on the woman's hand partway through. The video came from an Instagram account that posts AI-generated content.
