**Summary:** In the original ad, Gemini's text said Gouda makes up "50 to 60 percent of the world's cheese consumption." Bloggers pointed out that cheddar and mozzarella are far more widely eaten. Google first defended the line, saying it was grounded in the web and not a hallucination, then replaced it with a vaguer claim that Gouda is "one of the most popular cheeses in the world."
