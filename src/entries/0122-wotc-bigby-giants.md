From Wizards of the Coast's statement:

> "We are revising our process and updating our artist guidelines to make clear that artists must refrain from using AI art generation as part of their art creation process for developing D&D art."

**Summary (image slop described in text):** Illustrator Ilya Shkipin, who had worked on D&D books since 2014, used AI tools to finish several pieces for the giant-themed sourcebook. Fans flagged oddities such as a Frost Giant Ice Shaper with a strangely turned foot. Wizards said it had not known about the AI use, and later printings replaced the art with new human-made illustrations.
