From Wizards' since-deleted first reply:

> "This art was created by humans and not AI."

From its follow-up three days later:

> "Well, we made a mistake earlier when we said that a marketing image we posted was not created using AI."

**Summary (image slop described in text):** The image showed five retro-frame land cards in a steampunk laboratory. Viewers pointed out telltale AI errors in the background, such as nonsense numbers on a pressure gauge. Wizards blamed an outside vendor and "AI components" now built into tools like Photoshop, and said it would change how it works with marketing vendors.
