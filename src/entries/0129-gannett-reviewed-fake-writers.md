**Summary:** Reviewed's union said the shopping articles used repetitive, formulaic phrasing, and the bylined writers had little or no presence online. Gannett said the content came from freelancers hired by a marketing partner, and wasn't made with AI, though it acknowledged the pages lacked proper disclosures. The Verge found the partner also operated as AdVon Commerce, a company advertising "ML / AI solutions for E Commerce." AdVon was later linked to the fake-author Sports Illustrated pages in Exhibit 47.
