A phrase Futurism found in a large share of the guides:

> "Now, I know what you're thinking"

**Summary:** Futurism counted the same stock phrases and structures recurring across dozens of SEO travel guides credited to human writers "with help from" BuzzFeed's AI, describing destinations with interchangeable language like "hidden gem" and "a place where history and modernity coexist."
