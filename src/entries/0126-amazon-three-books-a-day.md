**Summary:** Amazon told KDP authors it was lowering publishing volume limits "to help protect against abuse," and that few publishers would be affected. It also began asking authors to disclose whether their books contained AI-generated content. The Register noted wryly that the limit still lets a single account publish three books every day. Exhibits 37, 38 and 42 in this museum show the kinds of books that prompted the limit.
