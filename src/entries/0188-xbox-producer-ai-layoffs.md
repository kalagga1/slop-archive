**Summary:** Matt Turnbull's post offered sample prompts for ChatGPT or Copilot covering résumés, networking and coping with impostor syndrome after a layoff. He wrote that he knew such tools could stir strong feelings but that they could help lighten the emotional load of losing a job. Game developers called the post tone-deaf, and it was deleted.
