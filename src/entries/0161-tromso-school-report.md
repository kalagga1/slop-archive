**Summary (sources are in Norwegian):** Norwegian broadcaster NRK found that many of the research sources cited in the report couldn't be found. The municipality admitted AI had been used and paused the public consultation. An external review by PwC later concluded the city hadn't been ready to use AI, citing weak guidelines, training and quality control. NRK later reported that an employee had asked the AI for sources supporting school closures.
