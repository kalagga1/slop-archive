**Summary (image slop described in text):** About 28 minutes in, a friend describes Jennifer Pan as bubbly and happy, over images that Futurism said had telltale AI distortions. Executive producer Jeremy Grimaldi told the Toronto Star the photos were real and that the background had been anonymized to protect the source. He didn't directly say whether AI tools had been used. 404 Media argued that altering images in a documentary creates a false historical record.
