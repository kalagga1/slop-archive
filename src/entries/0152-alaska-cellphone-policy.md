**Summary:** The Alaska Beacon found that the document posted for the state Board of Education cited studies, including some supposedly in real journals, that couldn't be located. Commissioner Deena Bishop said she used AI to create the citations for a first draft and believed she had corrected them before the board met, but a version with errors was still published. The board adopted the resolution anyway.
