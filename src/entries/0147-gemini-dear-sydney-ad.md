**Summary (video described in text):** The spot showed a dad proud of his track-star daughter, then prompting Gemini to "help my daughter write a letter" to her hero. Critics said it captured everything people dislike about AI: replacing the parts of life that are supposed to be personal. Google said the ad tested well before airing but phased it out of the Olympics rotation after the feedback. The video stayed on YouTube with comments turned off.
