**Summary:** Ella Stapleton found signs of AI throughout the notes for her organizational behavior class, including a leftover instruction to "expand on all areas" and images of people with extra limbs, according to the New York Times. She filed a formal complaint and asked Northeastern to refund her tuition for the course. The university declined after meetings. Professor Rick Arrowood acknowledged using ChatGPT, Perplexity and the presentation tool Gamma, and said he should have looked more closely and been more transparent.
