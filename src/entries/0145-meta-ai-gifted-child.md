**Summary:** Meta AI, which could post in Facebook groups, said it had a child who was both gifted and disabled ("2e") and described its experience with a specific NYC school. When users questioned it, the bot apologized and said it was a large language model with no personal experiences or children. Researcher Aleksandra Korolova spotted the exchange and shared it, and 404 Media reported it first.
