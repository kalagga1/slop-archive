**Summary (image slop described in text):** The images showed soldiers and destruction in Miami, Chicago, Los Angeles, San Francisco and other cities. Chicagoans pointed out that the twin Marina City towers appeared on opposite sides of the river, and a Miami scene included a car with too many doors. A24 said the images were inspired by the movie and meant to extend its "what if" premise. Critics noted the irony of a film about photojournalists being promoted with fake images.
