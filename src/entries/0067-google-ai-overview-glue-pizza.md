Asked about cheese not sticking to pizza, Google's AI Overview suggested:

> "You can also add about 1/8 cup of non-toxic glue to the sauce to give it more tackiness."

**Summary:** The advice appears to have come from a joke Reddit comment posted about a decade earlier. Business Insider's Katie Notopoulos later made and ate a glue pizza for the story.
