**Summary:** The system had taken more than two million orders, but clips of it accepting absurd prank orders, or repeatedly asking a frustrated driver what he'd like to drink, spread on social media. Taco Bell's chief digital and technology officer told the Wall Street Journal that the company was rethinking where voice AI helps, and that at busy restaurants a human might be better at taking orders. Exhibit 70 in this museum, McDonald's 260 Chicken McNuggets, ended its AI drive-thru test the year before.
