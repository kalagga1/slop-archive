**Summary:** In a lawsuit brought by a man incarcerated in an Alabama prison, Butler Snow attorneys representing the state cited cases that didn't exist or didn't say what the briefs claimed. U.S. District Judge Anna Manasco publicly reprimanded three attorneys, disqualified them from the case, and referred them to the Alabama State Bar. Her sanctions order said fabricated citations call for a stronger response than fines alone.
