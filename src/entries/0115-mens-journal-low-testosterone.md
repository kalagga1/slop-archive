**Summary:** Futurism asked Bradley Anawalt, chief of medicine at the University of Washington Medical Center, to review the article. He found at least 18 errors and unsupported claims, including mixing up low testosterone with hypogonadism and overstating links between diet and testosterone. After Futurism contacted the publisher, the article was heavily rewritten and an editor's note was added. The same publisher later ran the Sports Illustrated pages in Exhibit 47.
