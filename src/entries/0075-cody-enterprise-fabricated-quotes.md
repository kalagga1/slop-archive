**Summary:** Powell Tribune reporter CJ Baker noticed that stories by Cody Enterprise reporter Aaron Pelczar included plausible-sounding quotes that the quoted people, including Wyoming Governor Mark Gordon, said they never gave. A story previewing the Cody Stampede Parade ended with an explanation of the inverted pyramid style of news writing. Pelczar said AI may have generated the quotes and resigned; the Enterprise later found seven stories with AI-generated quotes from six people. Its correction stated:

> "Artificial Intelligence was allowed to misquote individuals in several of our articles … We regret the lack of oversight."
