**Summary (video described in text):** The 45-second spot showed a montage of holiday disasters, such as tangled lights, burned dinners and chaotic shopping, in an uncanny AI style. Commenters criticized the visuals and the bleak message. McDonald's Netherlands said it had wanted to reflect the stressful moments of the season but acknowledged that many customers see it as "the most wonderful time of the year." The production company defended the ad and the effort its team had put into it.
