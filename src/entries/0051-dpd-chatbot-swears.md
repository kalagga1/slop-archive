**Summary:** Ashley Beauchamp couldn't get any useful information from DPD's chatbot about his parcel, so he asked it to swear, criticise the company, and write poems. It obliged, calling DPD "the worst delivery firm in the world" and writing a poem that began:

> "There was once a chatbot named DPD,
> Who was useless at providing help."

DPD blamed "an error" after a system update and switched off the AI part of the bot.
