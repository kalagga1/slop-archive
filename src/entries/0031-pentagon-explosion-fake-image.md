**Summary (image described in text):** The image showed a plume of dark smoke beside an office building that didn't look like the Pentagon, with a fence that merged into the sidewalk. It was shared by verified accounts including one impersonating Bloomberg. The S&P 500 briefly fell before officials confirmed there was no explosion.
