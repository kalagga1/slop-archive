**Summary:** The books, with titles like *How to Write and Publish an eBook Quickly and Make Money*, imitated Friedman's subject matter. In a blog post titled "I Would Rather See My Books Get Pirated Than This," she described Amazon's response. After the post went viral, Amazon and Goodreads removed the titles. In September 2023 Amazon began limiting self-publishers to three new titles a day.
