**Summary:** Weixin Liang and colleagues estimated that between 6.5% and 16.9% of text in peer reviews submitted to several 2023–2024 AI conferences may have been substantially modified by LLMs. In ICLR 2024 reviews, "commendable," "meticulous" and "intricate" appeared 9.8, 34.7 and 11.2 times more often than before ChatGPT. LLM-flavored reviews were more common close to deadlines.
