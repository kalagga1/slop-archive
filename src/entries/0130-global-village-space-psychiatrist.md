**Summary:** The article claimed that a "Dr. Moshe Yatom," described as Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's psychiatrist, had died and left a note blaming his patient. NewsGuard traced it to a 2010 satirical post and found the site's text bore signs of being AI-rewritten. Full Fact found no evidence the psychiatrist existed. The claim spread widely on social media and in foreign media.
