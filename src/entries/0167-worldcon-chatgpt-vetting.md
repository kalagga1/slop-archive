**Summary:** The chair first said a person reviewed the ChatGPT results and that no one was excluded on AI results alone. After protests from authors, she apologized, and the convention said it would redo the vetting without generative AI. Hugo administrators Nicholas Whyte and Esther MacCallum-Stewart and division head Cassidy resigned, while saying AI hadn't been used in the Hugo Awards process.
