From Judge P. Kevin Castel's order:

> "Six of the submitted cases appear to be bogus judicial decisions with bogus quotes and bogus internal citations."

**Summary:** The invented authorities included *Varghese v. China Southern Airlines*, *Shaboon v. Egyptair* and *Martinez v. Delta Air Lines*, complete with made-up docket numbers and quotations. When opposing counsel couldn't find them, the attorney went back to ChatGPT, which assured him the cases were real and could be found in legal research databases.
