**Summary:** Users reported answers that drifted into Spanglish, made-up words and endless repetition, with some ending over and over in "Happy listening!" with a music emoji. OpenAI's status page called it "unexpected responses." Its post-mortem said an optimization had introduced a bug in how the model selected tokens on certain GPU configurations, producing "word sequences that made no sense." It was fixed the next day.
