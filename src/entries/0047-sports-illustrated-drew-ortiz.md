From the author bio of "Drew Ortiz":

> "Drew has spent much of his life outdoors, and is excited to guide you through his never-ending list of the best products to keep you from falling to the perils of nature."

**Summary:** Drew's headshot was listed for sale on a website of AI-generated faces. Arena Group blamed a third-party contractor, AdVon Commerce, and said the articles were human-written under pen names. Arena Group's CEO was fired weeks later, though the company did not tie the firing to the scandal.
