> "BEWARE!! There's a video out there promoting some dental plan with an AI version of me. I have nothing to do with it."

**Summary:** Hanks posted a screenshot of the fake, which showed a younger-looking digital version of him. He had said earlier that year that AI could keep actors "appearing" in films after their deaths.
