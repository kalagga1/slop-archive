**Summary (image slop described in text):** The images showed the hillside letters burning against an orange sky. Fact-checkers found telltale signs of AI, including generator watermarks and garbled lettering. The sign was intact, and the nearest fire was a few miles away. The fakes spread alongside real footage of the disaster, making it harder for residents to know what was happening.
