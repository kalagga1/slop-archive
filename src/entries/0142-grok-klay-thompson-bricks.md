**Summary:** Grok, which summarized trending topics on X, read basketball slang ("bricks" are badly missed shots) literally. Its story said Thompson had been accused of vandalizing houses with bricks and that authorities were investigating, and it noted that no one had been hurt. Ars Technica reported it as part of a pattern of Grok turning users' jokes into fake news.
