**Summary (image described in text):** Boris Eldagsen's black-and-white image shows two women from different generations, one gripping the other from behind, in the style of an old family photo. It won the Creative Open category. Eldagsen called himself a "cheeky monkey" who entered to see whether competitions were prepared for AI images, and said "they are not." The organizers said he had told them about AI "co-creation" before the win, accused him of deliberately misleading them, and removed him from the competition.
