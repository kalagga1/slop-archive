**Summary:** Current and former FDA employees said Elsa was useful for summaries and meeting notes but couldn't be trusted with important work because it invented or misrepresented research. The agency's head of AI acknowledged that Elsa could "hallucinate," like other generative AI. Commissioner Marty Makary said its use was optional.
