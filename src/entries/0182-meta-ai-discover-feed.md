**Summary:** Chats weren't public by default, but a Share button followed by Post published them to the Discover feed, sometimes linked to the user's Instagram name. Reporters found posts about health conditions, court cases and personal details, plus many voice recordings and odd image prompts. Meta added a warning that posted prompts are public and can show up elsewhere on its apps.
