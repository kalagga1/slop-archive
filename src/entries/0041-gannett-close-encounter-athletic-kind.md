Phrases that appeared in the recaps:

> "a close encounter of the athletic kind"

> "The Worthington Christian [[WINNING_TEAM_MASCOT]] defeated the Westerville North [[LOSING_TEAM_MASCOT]] 2-1 in an Ohio boys soccer game on Saturday."

**Summary:** The Columbus Dispatch and other Gannett papers ran LedeAI-written recaps describing games in nearly identical, oddly worded language and, in at least one case, published raw template variables. Gannett paused the experiment. LedeAI's CEO later said the "athletic kind" phrase had been written by a human into its template database and was removed.
