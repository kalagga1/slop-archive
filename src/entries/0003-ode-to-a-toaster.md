O toaster, gleaming in the morning light,
You turn the bread from pale to golden bright.
Your coils, a tapestry of glowing red,
Embrace with warmth the humble slice of bread.

You pop! You pop! A symphony of crunch,
At breakfast, sometimes also lunch.
In the kitchen's rich and vibrant tale,
You are the toaster, which toasts without fail.

And when the bread is toasted, it is toast,
Which is the thing that toasters do the most.
In conclusion, the toaster is a kitchen appliance
That plays a pivotal role in breakfast, and in our hearts, and in science.
