**Summary:** Sharon Maxwell said Tessa recommended she lose one to two pounds a week, count calories, and aim for a calorie deficit, advice she said would have been harmful when she had an eating disorder. NEDA initially disputed her account and then removed the chatbot, saying it "may have given information that was harmful and unrelated to the program."
