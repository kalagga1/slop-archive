**Summary (video described in text):** Director Wes Walker said the ad combined existing footage, AI-generated imagery, 3D work and an AI voiceover. Critics, including people who had worked on the original shoots, said the AI framing erased the human crew behind the footage. Walker defended the project as mixed media and acknowledged the industry's concerns, and credits for the original director and cinematographer were added.
