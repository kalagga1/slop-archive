**Summary:** A developer emailed Cursor support asking why they were logged out when moving between machines. A reply from "Sam" said this was expected behavior under a new policy limiting each subscription to one device. Users began cancelling subscriptions in protest on Reddit and Hacker News. A Cursor co-founder then explained there was no such policy, the logouts were a bug, and the reply had come from an AI support bot. Cursor said it would label AI responses going forward.
