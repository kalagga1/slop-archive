**Summary:** The employee was suspicious of an email asking for a secret transaction, but the video call with familiar faces reassured him. He made 15 transfers totaling HK$200 million to five bank accounts before checking with head office. Hong Kong police announced the case in February 2024, and in May the British engineering firm Arup confirmed it was the victim. It said fake voices and images were used and its internal systems weren't compromised.
