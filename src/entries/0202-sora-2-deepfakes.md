**Summary (video described in text):** The app's feed filled with fake security-camera footage, cartoon characters and clips of dead public figures, including Robin Williams, whose daughter Zelda publicly asked people to stop sending her AI videos of her father. One of the most shared clips was a fake CCTV video of OpenAI's own CEO, Sam Altman, shoplifting graphics cards from Target. OpenAI paused generations of King at the King estate's request and said representatives of other recently deceased figures could ask to block their likeness.
