Names generated by the network:

> "Stanky Bean"
> "Turdly"
> "Rose Hork"
> "Bank Butt"
> "Burf Pink"
> "Horble Gray"

**Summary:** Shane trained the network on about 7,700 Sherwin-Williams paint names and their colors. It often attached pleasant names to ugly colors and vice versa.
