**Summary:** A freelancer pitching as Margaux Blanchard sold articles to Wired, Business Insider and others. Press Gazette found the stories cited people and places that could not be verified. Wired published its own account of how it was fooled, saying the piece likely used AI and that it should have caught the problems. Business Insider removed two first-person essays after Press Gazette raised concerns.
