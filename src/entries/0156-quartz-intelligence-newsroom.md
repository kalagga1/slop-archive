**Summary:** TechCrunch found that the AI articles, mostly short rewrites of other outlets' reporting, carried a note admitting the experiment might not meet Quartz's standards for accuracy and timeliness. Futurism found articles that relied on Devdiscourse, a site that itself publishes AI-generated content, and an article on the stranded Boeing Starliner astronauts that got basic facts wrong.
