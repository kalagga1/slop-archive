**Summary (video described in text):** The pixelated characters, including Larry Feinberg, a stand-in for Jerry, wandered an apartment and talked in loops. When the creators switched from OpenAI's GPT-3 Davinci model to the older Curie model during an outage, moderation failed and the Larry character delivered a stand-up routine with transphobic jokes, resulting in a 14-day Twitch ban. It later returned with new safeguards.
