**Summary:** The "Summer reading list for 2025" in King Features' "Heat Index" insert recommended 15 books. Only five were real. Fake titles included *Tidewater Dreams* by Isabel Allende and *The Last Algorithm* by Andy Weir, each with a plausible plot description. Writer Marco Buscaglia told 404 Media he used AI for background and failed to check it: "Huge mistake on my part." The Sun-Times said it would not charge subscribers for the edition.
