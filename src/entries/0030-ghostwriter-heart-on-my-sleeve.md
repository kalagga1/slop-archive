**Summary:** "Heart on My Sleeve" used AI voice models to imitate Drake and The Weeknd and spread on TikTok, Spotify and YouTube. Universal Music Group issued takedown notices and the song was pulled from major platforms. The takedowns reportedly relied on a producer tag sample, since the cloned voices themselves raised unsettled legal questions.
