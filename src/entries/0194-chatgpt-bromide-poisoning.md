**Summary:** Doctors at the University of Washington wrote up the case of a 60-year-old man with bromism, a poisoning once common when bromide was used in sedatives. He said he read about salt's harms and asked ChatGPT about replacing chloride. When the doctors asked an older version of ChatGPT a similar question, its answer mentioned bromide, didn't give a specific health warning, and didn't ask why they wanted to know, which a medical professional would. He recovered after about three weeks of treatment.
