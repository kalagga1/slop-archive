**Summary:** "Fire and Fury: The Story of the 2023 Maui Fire and its Implications for Climate Change," credited to "Dr. Miles Stones," was listed as published August 10, 2023, two days after the fires began, and briefly ranked as a bestseller in its category. Reviewers said it read like AI-generated filler. A confusing listing date led some social media users to claim it was published before the fire, which fact-checkers debunked. Amazon removed it.
