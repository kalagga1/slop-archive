**Summary:** The Guardian had the AI-detection company Originality.ai check samples from several ADHD guides. It rated them as 100% likely to be AI-written. The books had generic author names, no credentials and confident advice on diet, focus and parenting. A researcher quoted by the Guardian called them dangerous because readers looking for help couldn't tell they weren't written by experts. Amazon said it has content guidelines and removes books that break them.
