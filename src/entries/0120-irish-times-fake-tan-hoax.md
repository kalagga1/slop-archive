**Summary:** The piece, credited to "Adriana Acosta-Cortez," argued that fake tan was cultural appropriation. After it was removed, editor Ruadhán Mac Cormaic apologized for a "breach of trust" and said the paper had been the target of a "deliberate and coordinated deception." He said the article and the byline photo may have been produced at least partly with generative AI, and promised stronger checks before publication.
