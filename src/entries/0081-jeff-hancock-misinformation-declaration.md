**Summary:** In a lawsuit challenging Minnesota's ban on deceptive election deepfakes, the state submitted a declaration from Stanford's Jeff Hancock. Plaintiffs' lawyers noticed it cited a 2023 study by "Hwang et al." that could not be found. Hancock acknowledged two fabricated citations and an error in author attribution, caused when he used GPT-4o to help draft. In January 2025 Judge Laura Provinzino excluded the declaration, writing: "The irony. Professor Hancock, a credentialed expert on the dangers of AI and misinformation, has fallen victim to the siren call of relying too heavily on AI."
