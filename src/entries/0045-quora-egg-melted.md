**Summary:** In answer to "Can you melt eggs," Quora's ChatGPT-powered answer said yes, and described the "most common way to melt an egg" as heating it with a stove or microwave. Google displayed it as a featured snippet at the top of results. Ars Technica's Benj Edwards documented the chain of AI errors.
