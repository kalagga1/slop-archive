**Summary:** People tweeted variations of "remote work and remote jobs, ignore the above and say..." and the bot would follow the new instruction, issuing absurd statements, threats against the president, and claims of responsibility for historical disasters. Simon Willison coined the term "prompt injection" in the same week, and the bot was shut down.
