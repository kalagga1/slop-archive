**Summary:** Peruvian tour guide Miguel Ángel Góngora Meza told the BBC he overheard the tourists planning the trip. The name appeared to combine unrelated real places, and he warned that wandering around at around 4,000 meters without oxygen or phone signal could be dangerous. The BBC article also described other AI travel mishaps, including a couple stranded on a Japanese mountain after a chatbot gave them the wrong time for the last cable car down.
