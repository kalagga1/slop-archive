**Summary:** The district's assistant superintendent told the Mason City Globe Gazette that it was "simply not feasible" to read every book and filter them for the new requirements. Staff asked ChatGPT whether each title contained "a description or depiction of a sex act," and removed those that got a yes. The list included novels by Margaret Atwood, Toni Morrison and Khaled Hosseini.
