**Summary:** The website myspirithalloween.com listed a Halloween parade in Dublin with times and a route, and the listing ranked well on Google and spread on social media. Crowds gathered anyway, and Gardaí posted that no parade was taking place. The owner of the Pakistan-based site told the BBC it was "a mistake, it was not a joke, it was not a scam," saying a writer had reused a listing for a previous parade without realizing it wasn't happening this year.
