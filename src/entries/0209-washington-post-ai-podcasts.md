**Summary (audio described in text):** Semafor reported that the AI hosts sometimes attributed quotes to people who never said them, inserted commentary presented as the paper's position, and mangled pronunciations. According to Semafor, internal testing found that between 68% and 84% of scripts failed to meet the Post's standards, but the product team went ahead, describing the feature as experimental. The app tells listeners to check the podcasts against the original articles.
