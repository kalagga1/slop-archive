**Summary:** U.S. District Judge Nina Wang identified close to 30 defective citations in a filing by Lindell's lawyers in the Coomer v. Lindell defamation case: misquotes, misrepresented holdings, and references to cases that do not exist. When asked in court whether the brief was AI-generated, lead attorney Christopher Kachouroff said he had used AI, and blamed the wrong draft being filed. In July 2025 the judge fined him and a colleague $3,000 each.
