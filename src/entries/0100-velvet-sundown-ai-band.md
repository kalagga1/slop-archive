**Summary:** The Velvet Sundown released two albums in June 2025 with AI-generated cover art and band photos. A man calling himself Andrew Frelon told Rolling Stone he was a spokesperson and the band used Suno, then said he was an impostor who had hoaxed the press. The band's Spotify bio was later updated to describe it as "a synthetic music project guided by human creative direction" and a "provocation."
