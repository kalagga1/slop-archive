After describing the food bank, the article advised:

> "Life is already difficult enough. Consider going into it on an empty stomach."

**Summary:** The piece, "Headed to Ottawa? Here's what you shouldn't miss!", appeared on Microsoft Start. Microsoft said the content was generated through a combination of algorithmic techniques with human review, and removed it.
