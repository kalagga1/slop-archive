**Summary:** In a housing case against a London council, a barrister's arguments cited cases that didn't exist. In a separate multimillion-pound claim against Qatar National Bank, many of the authorities cited were fictitious or misquoted, and the client admitted producing the research with AI tools. Dame Victoria Sharp, President of the King's Bench Division, wrote that AI tools can produce "apparently coherent and plausible responses" that are entirely wrong, and that lawyers who cite fake material risk regulatory referral, contempt proceedings or even police referral. The lawyers involved were referred to their regulators.
