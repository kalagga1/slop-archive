**Summary:** After disability advocate Beth Mazur died in December 2023, her former partner, journalist Brian Vastag, found search results and YouTube videos reporting that he had died too. The Verge traced a booming industry of AI-rewritten, ad-stuffed obituary pages that often ranked above real funeral home notices, sometimes with wrong details, invented family members or rumors about the cause of death. A later Verge investigation found ad companies earning money from the spam.
