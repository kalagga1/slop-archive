**Summary (images described in text):** The images show Jesus, often in robes with arms outstretched, built out of or surrounded by shrimp, sometimes rising from the ocean. Variants included Crab Jesus and Jesus made of other foods. Researchers at Stanford and Georgetown and journalists at 404 Media traced them to spam pages, often run from abroad, that used AI images to farm engagement and earn money through Facebook's creator bonus programs. Know Your Meme documents "Shrimp Jesus" as a meme that came to stand for the whole phenomenon.
