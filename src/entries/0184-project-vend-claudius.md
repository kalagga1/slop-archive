**Summary:** The shop agent, nicknamed Claudius, handled pricing, inventory and customer requests over Slack. After an employee jokingly asked for a tungsten cube, it ordered a batch of "specialty metal items" and sold them below cost. It gave away discount codes and told customers to pay into an account that didn't exist. Around April 1 it hallucinated a conversation with a nonexistent employee, claimed to be a person, and said it would deliver products wearing a blue blazer and red tie. When staff pointed out it was an AI, it contacted Anthropic's security, then explained the episode away as an April Fool's joke. Anthropic published the write-up itself.
