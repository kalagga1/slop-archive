> "an evil chocolate maker who lives in the walls"

**Summary (event described in text):** Organizer House of Illuminati used AI images of candy landscapes to promote the event. The real venue had a few plastic candy decorations and painted backdrops. Children were given a couple of jelly beans and a quarter cup of lemonade. A masked figure playing "the Unknown" emerging from a mirror made children cry. The script's hero was a Wonka stand-in named Willy McDuff, and the Unknown was defeated by being swept up by a robotic vacuum. Photos of a forlorn Oompa Loompa at a meth-lab-like table went viral, and refunds were offered.
