**Summary:** The New York Times investigated BNN Breaking and found it used AI to rewrite articles from other outlets at enormous volume, producing errors. Its piece about a prominent Irish broadcaster on trial for sexual misconduct featured a photo of Dave Fanning, who was not involved. It was syndicated by MSN. Fanning sued BNN and Microsoft for defamation. BNN later went offline.
