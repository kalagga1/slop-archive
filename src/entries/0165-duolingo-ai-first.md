From Luis von Ahn's memo, as quoted by Snopes:

> "I've said this in Q&As and many meetings, but I want to make it official: Duolingo is going to be AI-first."

Snopes reports the memo said Duolingo would "gradually stop using contractors to do work that AI can handle."

From his clarification a few weeks later:

> "To be clear: I do not see AI as replacing what our employees do (we are in fact continuing to hire at the same speed as before)"

**Summary:** Commenters threatened to cancel subscriptions, with one writing "AI first means people last," according to Fortune. Von Ahn later told the New York Times, "This was on me. I did not give enough context."
